3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • Authentication information
  • Account-related identifiers

Your password is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. We do not store your password in readable form.

3.2 Information You Create or Provide

Chief of Staff is designed to store information that you choose to enter, dictate, or create through the App.

This may include:

  • Tasks, including titles, descriptions, priorities, due dates, times, and completion status
  • Meetings, including titles, dates, times, attendee names, and notes
  • Notes, including titles, content, and tags
  • Contacts that you manually create, including names, roles, companies, and notes
  • Bills, including names, amounts, currencies, due dates, categories, recurrence, and payment status
  • Conversations and messages exchanged with Chief
  • AI-generated records or content created at your direction
  • Feedback, questions, or support requests that you voluntarily submit

Chief of Staff does not access or synchronize your device's native address book or contacts application. Contact information stored in Chief of Staff is information that you manually provide to the App.

3.3 Voice and Audio Data

If you use voice features, your spoken words are converted into text using the speech-recognition functionality available on your device.

Depending on your device and operating-system configuration, speech recognition may take place on the device or through a third-party speech-recognition service.

The resulting text may then be securely transmitted to our AI service provider, Anthropic, to interpret your request and generate a response.

When Chief responds using voice, the response text may be securely transmitted to our voice service provider, ElevenLabs, to generate spoken audio.

Chief of Staff does not intentionally record or retain your raw voice recordings as part of the App's normal operation.

3.4 Biometric Unlock

If you enable Face ID, fingerprint, or another biometric unlocking mechanism, the biometric verification is performed by your device's operating system.

Chief of Staff does not receive, view, or store your fingerprint pattern, facial geometry, or other underlying biometric information.

The App may retain a local preference indicating whether biometric unlocking has been enabled.

3.5 Technical and Diagnostic Information

We use Sentry, a third-party error-monitoring service, to help identify, diagnose, and resolve technical problems.

Depending on the circumstances, diagnostic information may include:

  • Device model
  • Operating-system version
  • App version
  • Installation-level identifier
  • Error information
  • Technical context relating to the operation of the App when an error occurs

We use this information primarily to maintain the stability, security, and reliability of Chief of Staff.

3.6 Information We Do Not Currently Collect

Chief of Staff does not currently request or intentionally collect:

  • Precise or approximate location information
  • Your device's native contacts or address book
  • Photos or videos from your device
  • Camera access
  • Payment-card numbers or bank-account credentials
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes

Chief of Staff does not currently operate an advertising network or sell personal data.
